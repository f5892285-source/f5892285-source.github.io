Privacy Policy

Personal Notes Bot is a private tool used only by its owner.

Data accessed: with the owner's consent, the tool accesses the owner's notebooks and sources in Gemini Notebook through the Gemini Notebook API, only to perform actions the owner requests.

Storage: OAuth credentials are stored only on the owner's own computer. Notebook content is not collected, sold, or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising.

Revoking access: the owner can revoke access at any time in the Google Account permissions page.
